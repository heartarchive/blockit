About Al-Ilm Noor Academy

A trusted home for Quranic learning, wherever you are

Al-Ilm Noor Academy, named for the saying “Knowledge is light”, is an online institute dedicated to teaching the Holy Quran, Tajweed, Arabic and Islamic Studies to students of every age and level. Our mission is simple: to make authentic, high-quality Islamic education accessible to every family, in a warm and respectful environment.

Every lesson is live and interactive. Your teacher follows a structured syllabus, tracks progress, and shares regular updates, so parents always know how their children are doing.

Classes & Fees

One-on-one or group: learn your way

Choose personal attention with a dedicated teacher, or learn alongside others in a small, focused group. Every plan starts with free trial classes.

One-on-One Classes

A private live session between you (or your child) and your own dedicated teacher.

  • Lessons fully paced to the student's level
  • Choose your teacher, days and time slot
  • Fastest progress, ideal for Hifz & Tajweed
  • Reschedule with 24 hours' notice

Group Classes

Small batches of 4–8 students of a similar age and level, led by an experienced teacher.

  • Separate batches for sisters, brothers & kids
  • Motivating, interactive class environment
  • Fixed weekly timetable, great for routine
  • Most affordable way to learn

Fees are monthly, in USD. Family discount: 10% off for each additional family member. Contact us for custom schedules.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to join the classes?

A laptop, tablet or smartphone with a stable internet connection and a headset. Classes are held over Zoom or Skype, and we'll help you set up before your first lesson.

How long is each class?

Classes are usually 30 minutes for younger children and 30–60 minutes for teens and adults. You choose the duration when selecting your plan.

Are the trial classes really free?

Yes. You get 3 free trial classes with no payment details required. You only choose a plan if you are happy to continue.

Can I choose a female teacher for my daughter or myself?

Absolutely. We have qualified female teachers for sisters and children, and male teachers for brothers. Just mention your preference in the enrolment form.

What's the difference between one-on-one and group classes?

One-on-one classes give the student a dedicated teacher and fully personal pace and schedule. Group classes have 4–8 students of a similar level on a fixed timetable, and are more affordable.

How do I pay the fees?

Fees are paid monthly in advance via bank transfer, PayPal or card. We'll send you full payment details after your trial.
